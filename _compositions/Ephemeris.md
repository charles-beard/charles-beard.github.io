Ephemeris is a musical work inspired by NASA’s recent Artemis II Mission. While observing the mission, I began to think about the differences between humans’ sensual and intellectual perceptions of celestial bodies; our senses reveal the sun and moon – our most perceivable celestial neighbors – as small and slow-moving, but through centuries of  science, we have come to understand that they are, in fact, very large, and travelling at enormous speeds. This piece musically charts a human’s process of rationalizing what we can perceive through the senses, with what we know to be factual – balancing internal and external sources of truth and knowledge.
