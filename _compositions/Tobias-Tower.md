Tobias' Tower was written in 2024 for <i>Hub New Music</i> as part of the Composition Seminar at Hamilton College. This piece takes a melody from a musical adaptation of a Norwegian children's story, and warps it into a horror-inspired, harsh, disorienting soundscape. This is my most directly programmatic work, following the movements of a character who is approaching Tobias' Tower – a place that looks unsuspecting, but is home to an unspeakable horror. During the approach, the character is calm at first, becomes apprehensive, then afraid, then catches a glimpse of hope before turning the knob and succumbing to complete teror.
<br><br>
Pieces written for the Composition Seminar were recorded in reading sessions, generally lasting from 20-30 minutes.
