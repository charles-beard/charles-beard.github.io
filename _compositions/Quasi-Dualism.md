Quasi-Dualism was composed in 2026 for <i>andPlay</i> as part of the Composition Seminar at Hamilton College. The piece was inpired by my fascination with the history of Catharism, a Christian religious movement in Southern France around the 13th century. The Cathars believed that there were two equally powerful Gods, one evil (of the Old Testament) and one good (of the New Testament). This piece is my interpretation of a dialogue between those two Gods. The evil God, represented by the violin, plays dissonantly and disruptively, while the viola, the good God, tries its best to convey profound, consonant ideas.
<br><br>
Pieces written for the Composition Seminar were recorded in reading sessions, generally lasting from 20-30 minutes.
