Acted upon was written in 2024 for members of <i>Anzû Quartet</i> as part of the Composition Seminar at Hamilton College. My first composition for a chamber ensemble, Acted Upon explores what it means to act upon a musical impulse, and distrubutes the agency to execute that impulse between the composer and performer. I combined traditional harmonic language with chance operations containing implied chromaticism to create a ternary piece that loosely resembles a descent into, and a subsequent escape from, madness.
<br><br>
Pieces written for the Composition Seminar were recorded in reading sessions, generally lasting from 20-30 minutes.
