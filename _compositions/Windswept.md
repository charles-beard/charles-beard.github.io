Windswept was written in 2026 for members of <i>Talea Ensemble</i> as part of the Composition Seminar at Hamilton College. It is a piece about facing adversity with grace. Experience has taught me that when facing hardship, one must be both firm and flexible: firm in identity and morality, but flexible in opinion and worldview. Willow trees have always been a sybol of these somewhat contradictory traits to me. in windswept, the piano represents the tree and the other instruemnts represent the storm; at times they blend to the point that they are indistinguishable, but the storm eventually passes, and the tree still stands.

Pieces written for the Composition Seminar were recorded in reading sessions, generally lasting from 20-30 minutes.
